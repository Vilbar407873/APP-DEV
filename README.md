# My Favorite Series: One Piece

![One Piece](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/6/65/One_Piece_Logo.png)

## About the Series
**One Piece** is a Japanese anime and manga series created by *Eiichiro Oda*. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew as they search for the legendary treasure called the One Piece.

## Why I Like It
I like One Piece because it has an interesting story, amazing characters, exciting battles, and meaningful lessons about friendship, dreams, and never giving up.

## My Favorite Characters
- **Monkey D. Luffy** – The captain of the Straw Hat Pirates.
- **Roronoa Zoro** – The swordsman of the crew.
- **Sanji** – The cook of the crew.
- **Tony Tony Chopper** – The crew's doctor.

## My Favorite Quote
> "I'm gonna be King of the Pirates!"

## Rating
**10/10** – A great series that I would recommend to everyone!
